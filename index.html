PERSONAL August 24, 2026

Piano

I've had a lot of ups and downs with piano. Some days I really don't want to practice anymore and I get so frustrated with it, but I always end up coming back to it. I think that's how I know it's something I actually love.

Right now I'm learning Lover, You Should've Come Over by Jeff Buckley, which I'm really excited about. I'm also learning Bach's Prelude in C Major, which is definitely harder than I expected, but I really love it.

I'm still not where I want to be with piano, but I'm trying to stick with it and keep getting better. I think I'll probably always have days where I hate practicing, but I can't really imagine not playing anymore.
